## Executive Summary

### Project Vision
To create the world's most effective alarm application specifically designed for adults with ADHD, combining ultra-reliable wake-up mechanisms with focus enhancement tools that address the unique neurological challenges of ADHD users.

### Business Justification
- **Market Gap:** Current alarm apps fail 67% of heavy sleepers with ADHD
- **Market Size:** 6.1 million adults in the US have ADHD (4.4% of adult population)
- **Revenue Opportunity:** $2.3M ARR potential based on comparable apps
- **User Pain Point:** Traditional alarms are insufficient for ADHD-related sleep/wake challenges

### Project Scope
**In Scope:**
- Ultra-loud alarm system with wake-up missions
- Social media and app blocking during focus periods
- Sleep tracking and correlation analytics
- Custom alarm sounds and scheduling
- Anti-uninstall protection mechanisms

**Out of Scope:**
- Medical diagnosis or treatment advice
- Integration with prescription medication tracking
- Therapy or counseling features
- Multi-user family management

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### High-Risk Items
1. **App Store Policy Violations**
    - Risk: Rejection due to accessibility service usage
    - Mitigation: Early policy review, alternative implementations prepared

2. **Android Fragmentation Issues**
    - Risk: Alarm failures on specific device manufacturers
    - Mitigation: Extensive device testing, manufacturer-specific optimizations

3. **User Privacy Concerns**
    - Risk: ADHD users sensitive to data collection
    - Mitigation: Privacy-first design, local data storage, transparent policies

### Medium-Risk Items
1. **Competition from Established Apps**
    - Risk: Market saturation, user acquisition challenges
    - Mitigation: Unique ADHD focus, superior user experience

2. **Technical Complexity Underestimation**
    - Risk: Timeline delays, budget overruns
    - Mitigation: Modular development, regular sprint reviews
